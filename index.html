注意力任务Attention Task

本任务用于测量反应速度和注意力，正式测试约2分钟。 This task measures your reaction speed and attention. The test takes about 2 minutes.

随机等待一段时间后，屏幕上会出现红色方块。 A red square will appear after a random waiting period.

手机／平板：看到红色方块后，请尽快轻触屏幕任意位置。 Phone / tablet: when the red square appears, tap anywhere on the screen as quickly as possible.

电脑：看到红色方块后，请尽快按空格键或点击屏幕任意位置。 Computer: when the red square appears, press the SPACE BAR or click anywhere on the screen as quickly as possible.

红色方块出现前，请勿点击或按键。 Do not respond before the red square appears.

你将先完成一次练习，练习反应不会被记录。 You will first complete one practice example. The practice response will not be recorded.
